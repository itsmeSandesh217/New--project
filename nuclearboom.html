DF-5 (CSS-4, Mod 1)

China began developing the Dong Feng 5in 1966, conducting its first limited test in 1971. After further development throughout the 1970s, the DF-5 completed its first full test flight in May 1980.2 The DF-5 entered operational service in 1981. The PLA deploys the missiles in hardened silos in central China. The DF-5 served as the basis for a number of other military and space programs. These efforts included the Long March-2C space launch vehicle.
